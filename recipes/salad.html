GREEK SALAD

Description

This Greek salad recipe is perfect for summer when tomatoes and cucumbers taste best. This salad tastes incredible!

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 (6 ounce) can pitted black olives
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese (not recommended if you ask me)
  • 6 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, olives, bell peppers, and red onion in a large bowl; sprinkle with feta cheese (if you choose to include it).
  2. Whisk olive oil, oregano, and black pepper together in a small bowl. Pour dressing over salad, toss well to combine, and serve.
  3. Pour dressing over salad, toss well to combine, and serve.
